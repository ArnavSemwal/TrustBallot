📋 How to use

  1. Start the kiosk: cd master-kiosk && npm run dev
  2. Open the kiosk in your browser and reach the QR scan step
  3. Open this HTML file on a second screen or phone
  4. Hold a QR code up to the kiosk camera — it will scan automatically
  5. After QR scan, continue to face-match (click Demo: Force Pass)
  6. Enter PIN 1234 (or duress PIN 9999) to complete voting
